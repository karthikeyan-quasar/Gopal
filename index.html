Save Gopal

One day, while walking down the streets, we came across someone who was just like us, yet his story was different. He was all alone, with no friends to accompany him and no one to share his journey with. Seeing him like that touched our hearts. We couldn't just walk away and leave him behind. So, we made a decision — to adopt him, give him the companionship he deserves, and take him along with us on our journey to Mysore. After all, no one deserves to be left behind when they can be part of a family. But this journey isn't something we can undertake alone. We need your support to make this little dream come true. To everyone visiting this page, we humbly request you to contribute whatever small amount you can. Every little contribution brings us one step closer to giving him a new beginning. Let's come together and make a difference in someone's life! JAI HIND! JAI GOPAL!
